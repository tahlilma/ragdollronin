HOW TO PLAY

Mouse swing your blade
AD move
Space jump / double jump
Shift dash (invincible)
Right-click plant the blade tip in stone
…then move the Mouse to pivot around it
WS shorten / lengthen grip
Space while planted: vault
S drop through ledges
F kick (no damage, huge shove)
Hold left-click charge iai · release to dash-cut
Esc pause · R restart · M mute
T taunt (online duel)
  • Your blade sweeps right through stone, so full 360° swings are fine. Hold right-click as the tip meets rock to bite it in and pivot.
  • Swing into an enemy's blade while it attacks to parry and stagger them. Holding still only blocks.
  • After a parry you have a brief riposte: your next hit is a guaranteed critical. Parry right as the swing starts for a perfect parry (longer stagger and window).
  • Kicks don't hurt, but they send people flying, and further the more hurt they are. Kick rivals into pits and spikes. A kick also breaks the Ronin's counter stance.
  • Iai draw-cut: hold left-click to sheathe and charge, then release to dash-cut toward the cursor (2 damage, or 3 at full charge). While charging you move slowly and can't swing or parry, and everyone can see it coming, so pick your moment.
  • Swat arrows, feathers and foxfire with a fast swing to send them back. Boulders need a very fast one.
  • Ki drains if you flail nonstop. Run dry and you're winded.
  • In online duels, the faster blade wins a clash and staggers the other samurai. Use a room code to duel privately with friends.
